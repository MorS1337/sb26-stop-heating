\section{What information is worth}
\label{sec:voi}

The observation gives one occupant count for the whole building, no per-zone occupancy and no
weather forecast, while comfort is charged per zone. We give the same controller each missing
channel in turn (Table~\ref{tab:voi}, Appendix~\ref{app:voi}) and never submit any of these
variants, so the differences measure what sensing would be worth. The oracles do not perturb
the simulation: dropping the extra input reproduces an ordinary run to the last digit, and the
forecast matches the sensor $h$ hours later to $1.4\e{5}$\,K.
% src: docs/voi_oracle.md §1 "Non-perturbation proof", "Forecast alignment"

\emph{Zonal occupancy is worth $2.9\,\%$.} Sending each zone to standby 15\,min after its own
occupants leave, instead of waiting for the whole building to empty, improves the dev30 score by
$2.86\,\%$ on both simulator versions and wins on all ten scenarios of the benchmark subset,
mostly by saving energy and carbon at almost no cost in comfort. Raising the release floor from
282 to 289\,K changes the result by less than $0.1\,\%$: what matters is \emph{which} zones are
empty, and hardly how cold they are kept.
% src: results/{voi_occ_dev30_shipped,voi_occ_dev30_corrected,voi_occ_mild,voi_occ_dev10_pc}; docs/voi_oracle.md §3

\emph{A perfect forecast adds nothing to a clock rule.} A fixed 2\,K pre-dawn lift, gated only on
the clock, the comfort-day flag and the zone sitting on its standby floor, improves dev30 by
$0.31\,\%$ and is neutral on the corrected simulator. Driving the same lift and the preheat
horizon from a perfect 12\,h forecast does slightly worse than the constant lift, so within our
resolution the forecast is worth nothing.
% src: results/{nightlift_dev30_shipped,nightlift_dev30_corrected,voi_fcst_dev30_shipped,voi_fcst_dev10_pc,voi_night_fixed,voi_fcst_night_lift}; docs/voi_oracle.md §3-4

\emph{Knowing the schedule is worth more than twice the sensor, and the difference is the lag.}
Per-zone foreknowledge of the episode's own occupancy, with a 5\,h preheat lead, improves the
subset by $6.0\,\%$ and the anchor by 2.6 units ($2.8\,\%$); the same information five minutes
late, as a sensor delivers it, gives up 3.7 of those 6.0 points.
