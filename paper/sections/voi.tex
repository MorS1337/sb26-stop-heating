\section{What information is worth}
\label{sec:voi}

The observation space exposes one occupant count for the whole building, no per-zone
occupancy and no weather forecast, while comfort is charged per zone. We hand the same
controller each missing channel in turn (Table~\ref{tab:voi}, Appendix~\ref{app:voi}) and never submit any of them, so
the deltas bound the value of sensing rather than propose an entry. The oracles are
transparent: dropping the payload reproduces an ordinary run to the last digit, and the
forecast matches the sensor $h$ hours later to $1.4\e{5}$\,K.
% src: docs/voi_oracle.md §1 "Non-perturbation proof", "Forecast alignment"

\emph{Zonal occupancy pays $2.9\,\%$.} Dropping each zone to standby 15\,min after its own
occupants leave, rather than waiting for the whole building to empty, improves dev30 from
0.0073694 to 0.0071585 and the corrected tree from 0.0071449 to 0.0069403 (both
$-2.86\,\%$); on the ten-scenario subset it is $-2.30\,\%$, better on 10 of 10, recovering
$23.9$ units of energy plus carbon for $0.16$ of comfort. Raising the release floor from 282
to 289\,K moves the subset score by $0.09\,\%$: the result is about \emph{which} zones are
empty, not how cold they get.
% src: results/{voi_occ_dev30_shipped,voi_occ_dev30_corrected,voi_occ_mild,voi_occ_dev10_pc}; docs/voi_oracle.md §3

\emph{A perfect forecast is worth nothing on top of a clock rule.} A fixed 2\,K pre-dawn lift,
gated only on the clock, the comfort-day predicate and the zone sitting on its standby floor,
scores 0.0073463 on dev30 ($-0.31\,\%$) and is a wash on the corrected tree (0.0071582 against
0.0071449). Driving that lift and the preheat horizon from a perfect 12\,h forecast scores
0.0073572: $0.15\,\%$ \emph{worse} than the constant lift on dev30 and $0.004\,\%$ worse on the
subset (the forecast-driven lift alone is $0.01\,\%$ better), so the information is worth zero to
within our resolution.
% src: results/{nightlift_dev30_shipped,nightlift_dev30_corrected,voi_fcst_dev30_shipped,voi_fcst_dev10_pc,voi_night_fixed,voi_fcst_night_lift}; docs/voi_oracle.md §3-4

\emph{The schedule is worth more than twice the sensor, and the difference is the lag.} Per-zone
foreknowledge of the episode's own occupancy, with a 5\,h preheat lead, scores 0.0112001 on the
subset ($-6.04\,\%$, 10/10) and $-90.52$ on the anchor ($+2.60$ units, $+2.79\,\%$); the same information
five minutes late, as a sensor gives it, loses $3.7$ points.
